NOOR DIFC, Dubai

Gate Village.

The vault door is open.

Raw concrete.

Black granite.

One candle.

There is no menu.

The kitchen decides.

The light overhead

moves from gold to rose.

Dried lime from Ras Al Khaimah.

Saffron harvested at dawn.

Camel milk from Al Ain.

Tea.
