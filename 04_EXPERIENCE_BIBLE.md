# EXPERIENCE BIBLE — Noor

**Selected Concept: THE CLOCK (Hypothesis A) with THE ABSENCE influence (Hypothesis B)**

---

## CONCEPT STATEMENT
The site presents a single evening at Noor — from 7:30pm to 11:15pm — as a temporal experience the visitor scrolls through. Time is the navigation axis. Photography is near-absent. Color temperature shifts through the scroll, mirroring the ceiling installation. The visitor feels duration, not information.

## MATERIAL WORLD
- **Primary material:** Light — warm amber shifting to cool blue-white and back
- **Secondary material:** Black granite — polished, reflective, heavy
- **Atmosphere:** The inside of a concrete vault at dusk, lit by a single candle and a shifting ceiling
- **Temperature:** Warm → cool → warm (follows the installation's arc)
- **Background color:** Not static. Shifts from deep warm black (#0D0907) at 7:30pm through cool near-black (#080A0D) at dessert, back to warm (#0D0907) at tea service
- **Text color:** Off-white that shifts warm/cool with the background (#F0E8DC warm, #E0E4EC cool)
- **Accent:** None. The color temperature shift IS the accent.

## SPATIAL WORLD
- **Light source:** Overhead, diffused, shifting color temperature. No lateral light. No shadows — only warmth and coolness.
- **Physics:** Weighted. Slow deceleration. Stone and light. GSAP power4.out.
- **Time signature:** Slow — 1.2–2.0s base. The site resists speed.
- **Boundary:** Infinite — no visible containers, no grid lines, no borders.

## WHAT THIS EXPERIENCE WOULD NEVER DO
- Show a conventional food photograph as hero
- List courses as a menu
- Include a chef biography section
- Use gold as an accent color
- Number sections
- Show press logos in a trust strip
- Use a standard reservation widget
- Play ambient video of service
- Use parallax on food photography

## SECTION ARCHITECTURE

### SECTION 1 — THE APPROACH (7:30pm)
**Purpose:** Establish the temporal frame. The visitor understands immediately that this is not a conventional site — it is an evening.
**User State:** Curious, slightly disoriented. "What is this?"
**Content:** Time display (19:30). Single line: "Gate Village. The vault door is open." Color: deep warm amber.
**Interaction:** Scroll begins. Time advances.

### SECTION 2 — THE ROOM (7:45pm)
**Purpose:** The visitor feels the space before seeing it.
**User State:** Settling in. Atmospheric immersion beginning.
**Content:** "Raw concrete. Black granite. One candle." A single generated atmospheric image — not the restaurant, but the feeling of it: warm light on dark stone. Extreme close-up texture, not a room photograph.
**Interaction:** Image breathes slowly (P70). Color temperature: warm amber.

### SECTION 3 — THE FIRST COURSE (8:00pm)
**Purpose:** The dining experience begins. Surrender as concept.
**User State:** Anticipation.
**Content:** "There is no menu. The kitchen decides." Sparse typographic moment — this is the manifesto beat. Large serif type, full viewport.
**Interaction:** Text reveals word by word on scroll. Each word earns attention.

### SECTION 4 — THE SHIFT (8:47pm)
**Purpose:** The emotional peak. Khalid's specific moment. The light changes.
**User State:** The visitor should feel the shift — not read about it.
**Content:** "The light overhead moves from gold to rose." The background color ACTUALLY shifts — the visitor's screen does what the ceiling installation does. This is the brave decision.
**Interaction:** Scroll-driven color morph. No text during the transition — just light changing. 3-second held pause.

### SECTION 5 — THE CRAFT (9:15pm)
**Purpose:** After the emotional peak, ground the experience in material specificity.
**User State:** Trust established. Now the visitor wants to know more.
**Content:** Brief, precise language about ancestral ingredients. "Dried lime from Ras Al Khaimah. Saffron harvested at dawn. Camel milk from Al Ain." Generated material texture images — not food photos, but the raw ingredients as materials.
**Interaction:** Minimal. Text appears cleanly. Color temperature cooling toward blue-white.

### SECTION 6 — THE SILENCE (10:30pm)
**Purpose:** The wind-down. Tea service. Return to warmth.
**User State:** Satisfied. Reflective.
**Content:** Almost nothing. "Tea." Background color shifts back to warm amber. A long pause with no content — just the return of warmth.
**Interaction:** Extended empty scroll space. The site breathes.

### SECTION 7 — THE DEPARTURE (11:15pm)
**Purpose:** The only transactional moment. Reservation.
**User State:** "I need to be there."
**Content:** "Noor. DIFC, Dubai." Single field or link for reservation inquiry. Not a booking widget — a contact point. The time reads 23:15.
**Interaction:** Minimal. The evening is over. The visitor acts or leaves.

## TYPOGRAPHY
- **Display:** A single serif with extreme weight contrast — suggest Romie or Editorial New (if available), else Cormorant Infant at weight 300
- **Body:** No body font. All text is display-scale. There is very little text.
- **Scale:** Hero time display at 20vw+. Section text at 5–8vw. Nothing smaller than 3vw.
- **Tracking:** Tight on time display (-0.04em). Open on descriptive text (+0.02em). The tracking shifts with the temperature.

## MOTION
- All motion is scroll-driven. No timed animations.
- Color temperature interpolates continuously on scroll progress.
- Text reveals: per-word, tied to scroll position.
- Image breathing: 16s ease-in-out scale cycle (P70).
- No parallax. No stagger. No entrance animations. Stillness is the default.
- Film grain active throughout (P52), opacity shifts warm/cool with temperature.

## AUDIO (optional, brave)
- Barely audible room tone: silverware, low conversation hum, silence.
- Shifts with scroll position. Arrives at THE SHIFT section.
- Volume maximum: 0.03. The visitor should feel it, not hear it.
- Muted by default. Unmute icon in corner.
