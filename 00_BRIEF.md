# CLIENT BRIEF — Noor

## The Restaurant
Noor is a 42-seat fine dining restaurant in DIFC, Dubai. The name means "light" in Arabic. Chef Khalid Al-Rashid trained at Noma (Copenhagen) and Gaggan (Bangkok) before returning to Dubai in 2023. The cuisine is modern Emirati — ancestral ingredients (camel milk, dates, saffron, dried lime) prepared with Nordic technique.

## What Makes Noor Different
Khalid does not serve a menu. There is a single 12-course tasting experience that changes with the lunar calendar. Each new moon, a new sequence. Guests do not choose — they surrender to the kitchen's judgment. The dining room has no art on the walls. The only decoration is light itself — a ceiling installation by Emirati artist Shaikha Al Mazrou that shifts color temperature through the meal, from warm amber (arrival) to cool blue-white (dessert) to amber again (tea service).

## The Space
Former jewelry vault in Gate Village, DIFC. Raw concrete walls. Terrazzo floors. 14-foot ceilings. No tablecloths — plates rest on polished black granite slabs. Each table has exactly one candle. The kitchen is partially visible through a long horizontal window.

## Current State
Open 8 months. 3-month waitlist. Covered by The National, TimeOut Dubai, Condé Nast Traveller Middle East. One Michelin star (awarded June 2024). Price: AED 1,200 per person (~$327 USD). No photos allowed during service — this is policy, not suggestion.

## What Khalid Wants
"I don't want a restaurant website. Every restaurant website looks the same — hero photo, menu, about the chef, book a table. I want someone to visit the site and feel what it is like to sit in my dining room at 8:47pm when the third course arrives and the light overhead shifts from gold to rose. If the website cannot do that, I would rather have no website."

## Target Audience
- Dubai residents and visitors with AED 5,000+ monthly dining budget
- Food-obsessed travellers who plan trips around restaurants
- The type of person who has eaten at Noma, Eleven Madison Park, Mirazur
- Age: 28–55. They do not need to be told what fine dining is.

## Competitors (in Khalid's words)
"Everyone in DIFC has the same website. Dark background, gold text, food photos with smoke. I see Zuma's site, I see La Petite Maison's site, I see Nobu's site — they are all the same site with different logos. I want something that makes those sites look like templates."

## Budget
Not stated. Khalid said: "I spent two years finding the right stone for the tables. I will spend whatever is necessary for the right website."
